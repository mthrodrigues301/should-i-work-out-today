# My Workout — Instagram carousels

Each folderless sequence is ready for an Instagram portrait carousel (1080 × 1350 px), in this order:

- `en-01-cover.png` through `en-05.png`: English
- `bilingual-01-cover.png` through `bilingual-05.png`: English and Brazilian Portuguese
- `ptbr-01-cover.png` through `ptbr-05.png`: Brazilian Portuguese

The source art is `my-workout-key-art.png`; the final slides were composed by `scripts/create-workout-carousels.mjs`.
